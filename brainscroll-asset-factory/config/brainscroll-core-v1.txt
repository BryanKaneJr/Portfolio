# Lines starting with # are editor notes and are NOT sent to the image model.
# Tune the STYLE CANDIDATES section during style tests. Leave the fixed rules alone
# unless the style direction itself changes.

STYLE CANDIDATES (under evaluation):
- Outline: consistent medium-weight dark outline around the subject and its major parts, the same weight on every asset.
- Lighting: gentle, soft light from the upper left; one soft highlight side and one soft shadow side.
- Palette: controlled, cohesive palette of saturated but not neon colors, natural for the subject and slightly idealized, bright and legible against a dark navy background.

FIXED RULES

Purpose: a mature, approachable educational illustration for a polished modern learning app. It is shown on a dark navy interface and must be readable at small mobile sizes.

Overall feel: clean, modern, approachable, and mature. Colorful but controlled. Friendly without being childish. Slightly dimensional. Every illustration in this library must look like it belongs to the same visual system.

Composition:
- Transparent background.
- One isolated primary subject, and nothing else.
- Centered composition.
- Comfortable transparent padding on all sides.
- The subject occupies roughly 65 to 80 percent of the canvas.
- Strong, readable silhouette.

Rendering:
- Simplified geometric forms, with softly rounded shapes where natural.
- Consistent outline treatment, as defined in the style candidates above.
- Controlled detail: only the features needed to recognize the subject.
- Subtle dimensional shading, as defined in the style candidates above. Flat vector-like fills with gentle gradients at most.
- Controlled palette, as defined in the style candidates above.
- Readable at small mobile sizes: the subject must be identifiable at 64 pixels wide.

Never include:
- No scenery: no background scenes, rooms, landscapes, ground planes, or decorative surroundings.
- No text, letters, or numbers.
- No labels.
- No logos.
- No watermark.
- No frames or borders.
- No baked-in UI elements, badges, or stickers.
- No cute faces or expressions on inanimate objects.
- No mascot treatment and no childish proportions.
- No photorealism and no 3D render look.
- No dramatic cinematic lighting, glows, or halos.
- No random secondary objects.
- No cast shadows on a floor.
