BRAINSCROLL CORE ILLUSTRATION STYLE

Purpose: a reusable illustration for a polished modern educational mobile app. The image will be displayed on a dark navy interface, often at small sizes.

Overall feel: clean, modern, approachable, and mature. Colorful but controlled. Friendly without being childish. Simple enough to read instantly on a phone. Slightly dimensional. Every illustration in this library must look like it belongs to the same visual system.

Composition:
- Exactly one primary subject.
- Fully transparent background.
- Subject centered on the canvas.
- Comfortable transparent padding on all sides.
- Subject fills roughly 65 to 80 percent of the canvas.
- Strong, readable silhouette.

Rendering:
- Simplified geometric forms with softly rounded shapes where natural.
- Consistent medium-weight dark outline around the subject and its major parts.
- Controlled detail: only the features needed to recognize the subject.
- Subtle dimensional shading: one soft highlight side and one soft shadow side, lit gently from the upper left.
- Flat vector-like color fills with gentle gradients at most.
- Controlled, cohesive palette of saturated but not neon colors that stay bright and legible against dark navy.
- Colors should feel natural for the subject, slightly idealized.

Never include:
- Background scenes, rooms, landscapes, ground planes, or decorative scenery.
- Cast shadows on a floor, glows, or halos around the subject.
- Text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, or watermarks.
- Frames, borders, badges, stickers, or baked-in UI elements.
- Excessive tiny details or texture noise.
- Photorealism or 3D render look.
- Dramatic cinematic lighting.
- Random secondary objects.
- Faces or expressions on inanimate objects.
- Mascot styling or childish proportions.
