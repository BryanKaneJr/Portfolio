# Mastery images with Dr. Scroll (all 10 new trees)

Every tree has two mastery images:

- **UI badge** (`ui/mastery-<tree>`): the tree's gold emblem on its own. Shown on the Skills tab and trophies.
- **Level 100 art** (`<prefix>.mastery`): Dr. Scroll holding up that same emblem, awarding it to the learner. Shown on the Mastery Challenge.

Only the Level 100 art needs Dr. Scroll: one per new tree, ten in all. Each holds the same emblem as the tree's UI badge.

**Same for all ten:** Dr. Scroll exactly as in the library (bald on top, white tufts at the sides, round dark glasses, brown suit, cream shirt, purple bow tie). Standing, full body, big smile, one arm raised high, holding the emblem up beside his head. The emblem is the only gold. Glossy 3D clay, soft light from the top left, transparent background, 1024 × 1024 PNG, no text, letters or numbers. Match `gov.mastery` and `technology.mastery`.

| `mind_logic-mastery.png` | Logic & Critical Thinking | Gold puzzle piece |
| `mind_probability-mastery.png` | Probability & Statistics | Gold die |
| `mind_psychology-mastery.png` | Psychology | Gold brain, smooth and stylized, no labels |
