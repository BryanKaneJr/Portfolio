## Needs your call
### 3. Level 15: Percussion  **SOFTEN**
> They're usually played in pairs, tuned to different pitches.

**Problem:** Pairs were the norm in the Baroque and Classical orchestra, but a modern orchestral timpanist usually plays a set of four drums, so "usually in pairs" leaves the learner with a dated picture. The "only drums tuned to definite pitch" part is fine.
**Proposed:** "They're played in sets of two or more, each tuned to a different pitch; today's standard set has four."  (appears in: card 015.c4 body)
**Evidence:** [Timpani (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timpani): "A standard set of timpani (sometimes called a console) consists of four drums." The cited [Britannica Kettledrum](https://www.britannica.com/art/kettledrum) does say "usually played in pairs", so this is a judgment call on how current the card should be.
