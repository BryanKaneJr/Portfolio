# Architecture: tree rules
- Structure explained correctly (loads, compression, tension, thrust); analogies labeled.
- Records (tallest, largest, longest) name the holder, the year and the source (CTBUH for heights).
- Attributions and dates from Britannica, UNESCO, national heritage bodies (English Heritage, Historic England, the NPS), museum and university pages, RIBA, ASCE.
- Overlaps: Everyday Technology Levels 81–90 (skyscrapers, elevators, bridges, concrete, tunnels, steel), Egypt Chapter 3 (pyramids), Middle Ages Chapter 3 (castles), Art History (cathedrals), Ancient Greece (column orders). Do not contradict them; keep the architecture angle (design, form, how it's built, what it means).
- The Taj Mahal, Machu Picchu, the Alhambra etc.: who built them, for whom, and how, with sourced dates; avoid romantic legends stated as fact (label them).
