# Music: tree rules
- Sound physics correct (frequency, harmonics, resonance); music theory accurate.
- History from Britannica, Grove (via university library summaries), the Library of Congress, the Smithsonian (Folkways, NMAAHC), university music departments.
- Respectful, accurate treatment of every tradition: no exoticism, no "primitive" framing; name specific traditions, instruments and places. African American origins of spirituals, blues, jazz, rock and roll and hip-hop are stated clearly.
- Composers' lives told accurately (e.g., Beethoven's deafness progression; the Mozart-Salieri rivalry myth labeled as myth).
- Industry figures (streaming payouts, sales) are dated and sourced, presented as figures reported by named bodies.
- Music and the brain: sourced studies; no "Mozart effect" claims; perfect pitch figures from studies.
