# Oceans: tree rules
- Science from NOAA, NASA, USGS, WHOI, MBARI, the Smithsonian Ocean portal, Britannica, IPCC for climate.
- Climate science (warming, acidification, sea-level rise) stated as the scientific consensus with IPCC/NOAA figures and years; policy debates, if any, as named views.
- Records (deepest dives, biggest tides, largest waves) name the holder, date and source.
- Overlaps: World Geography (water chapter, plate tectonics, volcanoes), Animals Chapter 5 (fish, sharks, deep sea, octopus, coral, tide pools, ocean food webs), Everyday Technology (ships, sonar, submarines). Do not contradict them; recall rather than re-teach, and keep the ocean-as-a-place angle.
- The Titanic and pirates: accurate history, not legend.
