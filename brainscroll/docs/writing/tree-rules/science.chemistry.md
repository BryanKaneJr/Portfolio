# Chemistry: tree rules
- Physically and chemically correct; analogies labeled as analogies; exact values with units and a source (NIST, IUPAC, Britannica, OpenStax Chemistry 2e, RSC, ACS, LibreTexts, university pages).
- Safety: never give instructions for experiments, synthesis, energetic materials or mixing chemicals. Explosions, fireworks, acids and cleaning products are explained conceptually only. "Cleaning Safely" may state widely published public-safety guidance (never mix bleach with ammonia or acids) attributed to health agencies, without describing reactions in actionable detail.
- Element counts: 118 confirmed (IUPAC). Radioactivity and carbon dating: correct half-lives, with sources.
- Chemistry myths and history (alchemy, Lavoisier, Curie, Rosalind Franklin's role in DNA structure) told accurately and fairly.
- Overlaps: Everyday Technology (batteries, plastics, fuels) and Human Body (digestion, enzymes). Keep the chemistry angle; do not contradict them.
