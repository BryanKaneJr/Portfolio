Fat Bat

Fat Bat is hungry. Hold to flap, let go to drop, and steer him through a twisting cave, eating every glowing bug in his path. The more he eats, the fatter he gets, and the harder he is to fly.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Hold anywhere on the screen to flap, and let go to drop. Eat the bugs and don't touch the rock. Rare bugs are worth more, and every bug counts for more the fatter he gets (up to 5 times as much at Absolute Unit).

How do I get new hats and outfits?

Every run's score goes into your Bug Bank. Spend it in the Wardrobe. Everything in the game is earned by playing; there are no in-app purchases.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone. If you delete the app, your Bug Bank, outfits and best score are deleted with it.

How does the leaderboard work?

The World's Fattest Bats leaderboard uses Apple's Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings, then tap FATTEST BATS in the game. Your best score is sent after each run.

There's no sound.

Fat Bat follows your iPhone's silent switch. Flip it off silent, or turn the volume up. The game also has its own sound button in the top corner.

Does Fat Bat have ads?

No. The results screen sometimes shows our other games, BrainScroll and Poke & Tell, with a link to the App Store. You never have to download them, and nothing in Fat Bat depends on it.
