This policy explains what happens to information when you play Fat Bat.

We don't collect your personal information. Fat Bat has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't sell or share any information about you.

Your game progress stays on your iPhone. Your Bug Bank, outfits, settings and best score are stored only on your device. They aren't sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center. If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best score is sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboard, where other players can see it with your Game Center nickname. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy (apple.com/privacy). We can see the leaderboard's scores and nicknames, but nothing else about you.

Sharing your result card. When you tap SHARE, your iPhone's share sheet opens with a picture of your run. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it. If you choose "Save Image", the app asks for permission to add the picture to your Photos. It can only add pictures; it can't see your photos.

Links to the App Store. The game links to our other games on the App Store. When you open one, Apple may tell us, in anonymous totals, how many people downloaded a game after coming from Fat Bat. We never receive information that identifies you.

Children. Fat Bat is a general-audience game and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
