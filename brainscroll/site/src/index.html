How it works

A level a day, and it actually sticks

Learn in levels

Each level is a few short cards that make one idea click, then three questions. Real ideas, explained well, no filler.

Get it wrong, then get it right

Your first try earns the XP. Miss one and you see the card that explains it, then try again until it's yours.

Remember it for good

Smart review brings ideas back just before you'd forget them, so what you learn this week is still there next year.

Your brain, leveled up

Watch everything you know grow

Every level you clear raises your brain level and fills your subject ring. Climb a tree from Level 1 to 100 to master it and earn its gold star, collect trophies along the way, and keep a streak going on the days you learn.

Learn with friends

A weekly league at your level

Add friends with a link or a username. Every week you join a league of up to 20 learners near your brain level, and the top three win bonus XP. Cheer each other on with Dr. Scroll reactions. No chat, no comments, just learning.
