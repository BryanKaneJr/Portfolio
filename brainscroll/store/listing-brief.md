# BrainScroll: App Store listing brief

This file is for an AI (or a copywriter) writing BrainScroll's App Store listing. It says what the app is, who it's for, everything it does, the rules the copy must follow, and the current draft to improve on. Everything under "What the app does" is true of the app today; don't add features that aren't listed.

## 1. What to write

Write the App Store Connect listing, in American English:

## 2. Rules the copy must follow

These are hard rules (Apple's or ours). A listing that breaks one can't be used.

- **No em dashes (the long dash, U+2014) anywhere.** Use a comma, colon, period or parentheses instead. This is a house rule for everything BrainScroll writes.
- **Only claim what the app does** (sections 4 to 7). No "#1", "best", "proven", "science-backed", "used by thousands", ratings, awards, testimonials or invented statistics. Numbers you may use are in section 5.
- **Don't name other apps, companies or platforms** (no Duolingo, TikTok, Instagram, Brilliant, Wikipedia and so on). "Doomscrolling" and "endless feed" are fine.
- **Brainpower** is BrainScroll's pacing. Describe it as the app does: each new level uses 1, you refill to 5 every day, you can hold up to 10, and you earn more by learning. Never call it energy, lives or hearts, and never suggest wrong answers cost it (they don't).
- **Unlimited** (the subscription) gives unlimited Brainpower and nothing else. Never imply it unlocks content, subjects, XP, trophies or "premium lessons". Every level can be unlocked free over time.
- **Subscription disclosure:** the description ends with the auto-renewal paragraph in section 10 (keep it word for word). The privacy policy and terms links go in their own App Store Connect fields.
- **No chat, messages, comments or public posts exist.** The social side is friends, weekly leagues, a world leaderboard and a feed of achievements with a heart button.
- **iPhone only** (no iPad version). An account is required (Sign in with Apple, or Google). It needs an internet connection.
- **Notifications are optional.** The daily reminder is off until the learner turns it on; friend and league notifications can be switched off. The app never guilt-trips: no streak threats, no countdowns, no "you'll lose your streak".
- **Not for young children.** Don't pitch it to kids or parents of kids; it's for teens (13+) and adults.

## 3. Voice

- The tagline is **"Stop scrolling. Start leveling."** The app's own sign-off when your Brainpower runs out is **"No more doomscrolling. Go touch grass."**
- Clever, self-aware, a little irreverent, never preachy. Warm, plain words; short sentences. Confident, not hypey.
- Learning first: the point is that you actually know more. Game words (level, XP, quest, league) are the fun wrapper, not the product.
- Respect the learner's time: BrainScroll is the app that tells you when you're done for the day.

## 4. What BrainScroll is

**In one sentence:** a learning app shaped like the apps you scroll, where every session is a short, finished lesson in a real subject, and you level up real knowledge the way a game character levels up stats.

**The problem it answers:** people lose hours to endless feeds and come away with nothing. BrainScroll keeps what's satisfying about scrolling (quick, visual, one more thing) but every swipe is part of a real curriculum, it ends, and you leave knowing something.

**What makes it different:**

- **Finite and authored, not an endless feed.** Each skill is a fixed curriculum of 100 levels, in order, from the basics to the good stuff. Level 63 of Astronomy means something specific.
- **A character sheet for your brain.** Skill levels, subject levels, an overall Knowledge Level, Mastery stars and trophies show what you actually know, e.g. "Astronomy Lv. 84".
- **Learning, not trivia.** Each level teaches one idea with short cards, then checks it with three questions. It isn't a quiz game; questions exist to lock in what you just learned.
- **Wrong answers teach.** Miss a question and "Take another look" shows you the card that explains it, then you try again. No lives, no failure screen, no starting over.
- **Free forever, paced on purpose.** Brainpower refills every day, and when it's used up the app tells you you're done instead of trying to keep you hooked. Review is always free and unlimited.
- **No ads, no tracking, no selling your data.**
- **Checked content.** Lessons are written in advance and their facts checked against published sources before release. The app never makes up facts on the fly.

## 6. What the app does

**A level.** A hook, then 2 to 5 short learning cards that make one idea stick (with an illustration and a "Key idea"), then 3 questions: recall, understanding and connection. Question types: multiple choice, fill in the blank, match the pairs, and put in order (drag). Levels come in chapters of 10, each with a title (e.g. "Your Place in the Universe"). Every 10th level is a checkpoint that recaps the chapter ("You know this now"); Level 50 is a milestone; Level 100 is the Mastery Challenge, which earns the skill's Mastery star.

**Progress.** XP for every level (your first try sets it, so it rewards knowing, not guessing until right). Clearing a level raises the skill's level; subjects rank up with their skills, and an overall Knowledge Level reflects it all. Perfect levels in a row pay a little extra. The profile shows a ring of all six subjects and your stats.

**Review.** Concepts you've learned come back on a schedule, sooner if you missed them (spaced repetition). You can also go back over any finished chapter. Review is unlimited and never costs Brainpower.

**Trophies.** 42 trophies for real milestones: your first level and first chapter, perfect lessons, knowledge milestones, every skill in a subject mastered, and Master of All (every skill to Level 100). Streak trophies (7, 30, 100, 365, 500 and 1,000 days) are earned by your longest run and never taken away. Any trophy can be shared as an image.

**Learning streak.** A day counts when you learn or review. Extending it earns +1 Brainpower. Missing a day costs nothing except the run itself.

**Weekly Quests.** One themed quest each week (52 written, starting the week of 12 October 2026), e.g. The Roman World, How We Think, The Restless Planet. Each ties several skills into one theme: learn new levels across them, then a Final Round. Finish within its week for the quest's trophy and a title for your profile; past quests stay playable in the Archive.

**Leagues.** Each week you're grouped with up to 20 learners, ranked by XP earned that week. The top 3 win 1,000, 500 and 250 bonus XP; the top 5 move up a tier and the bottom 3 move down. Eight tiers: Quartz, Amethyst, Aquamarine, Sapphire, Emerald, Ruby, Diamond and Crown.

**World leaderboard.** Everyone ranked by all-time total XP; it never resets. Your rank shows on your profile.

**Friends and feed.** Add friends by invite link or exact username. A feed shows what friends achieve (trophies, finished chapters, streaks, league wins and promotions) with a heart button; there are no comments or messages. Every profile can be blocked or reported.

**Map chests and looks.** Each chapter's map has a chest to open once: it can hold Brainpower, an XP boost (double XP for 15 to 60 minutes) or a cosmetic (a glow around your avatar, a name style or a title). Chests are never sold.

**Dr. Scroll.** The mascot: a cheerful old genius in a brown suit and violet bow tie. He points out key ideas, cheers level-ups, shrugs kindly at a miss and turns up in costume on the skill maps.

**The look.** Dark, colorful and game-like: each subject has its own color, the skill map is a winding road of hexagon levels with scenery, and the tabs are illustrated icons (Practice, Leagues, Home, Social, Profile).

**Notifications.** An optional daily reminder at a time you choose (off until you turn it on), plus friend and league notifications you can switch off. Never guilt trips.

**Account.** Sign in with Apple (or Google); progress lives in your account and follows you to any iPhone. Delete your account from inside the app.

## 7. The deal (pricing)

- **Free, forever.** Each new level uses 1 Brainpower. You refill to 5 every day and can hold up to 10. You earn more by keeping your streak, winning trophies, finishing a chapter review, working on the Weekly Quest, opening map chests and, sometimes, a perfect level. Review, replays and wrong answers never cost anything. When it's used up, the app tells you, and you can go do something else.
- **BrainScroll Unlimited:** $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year (works out to about $3.33 a month), with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. It gives unlimited Brainpower: as many new levels a day as you like. That's all it changes. Everyone earns the same XP, levels, stars and trophies, and all knowledge can be unlocked free over time.
- No ads, ever. No coins, gems or loot boxes for sale.

## 8. Who it's for

Firm facts: made for teens (13+) and adults; the audience is mostly American (US History is in the catalog; the other skills are world-wide). Beyond that, these are the people we built it for, so the listing should speak to them:

- **People who want their scrolling time back.** They know they lose an hour a night to feeds and want a swap that leaves them smarter, not guilty. This is the core audience; the anti-doomscrolling angle is the hook.
- **Curious generalists and lifelong learners.** They watch documentaries, listen to history and science podcasts, fall down Wikipedia holes and like knowing how things work.
- **People who want to follow the news with a clear head:** money and inflation, government and elections, AI and technology, statistics, logic.
- **People who enjoy games and progress.** Levels, XP, streaks, leagues and trophies are what keep them coming back, so the game layer matters, as long as it reads as fun around real learning.
- **Trivia and quiz fans**, with care: they're welcome, but BrainScroll teaches first and quizzes second, so pitch it as "learn it, then prove it", not as a trivia game.

Not the audience: young children, exam prep, language learning.
