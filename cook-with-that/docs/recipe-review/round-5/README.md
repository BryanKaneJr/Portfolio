# Round 5 review (2026-10-11)

The owner decided the recipes won't be cook-tested (CLAUDE.md decision 18), so this paper review is the last check.
Six reviewers read the 120 round-5 recipes (one theme file each) as cooks; a seventh checked the 20 older recipes that
the new `ratio` audit check flagged. The brief is the same as the first review's (`../README.md`), with decisions 11–18.
Each reviewer's notes are in this folder (`rv5-*.md`).

## What changed

- **36 of the 120 new recipes fixed**, none dropped. The fixes were small:
  - **Yields that didn't match the instructions:** cookie, truffle and biscuit portions, and a 6-by-9 rectangle that
    couldn't be cut into 9 squares.
  - **Pans too small:** a brim-full baking dish, a 12-inch skillet for an 11-cup toss, a 2-quart pot for rice and
    peas, a frying pot too small for 2 inches of oil.
  - **Step order:** dressing made "while the eggs cook" after they were peeled, tortillas warmed while eggs sat in a
    hot pan, a missing oven preheat for the biryani's oven option.
  - **Rice and peas:** enough liquid for the rice to cook through.
  - **Frying safety:** the oil comes back to temperature between batches, and the oil depth is capped at half the pot.
  - **Honest times and taglines:** cook times that skipped bringing water to a boil, taglines promising "salt-crusted"
    or "20 minutes" when the recipe isn't.
- **Provenance:** the desserts reviewer recognised two formulas as published ones, from memory. The writers hadn't
  looked anything up, but the rule is never to copy one source's exact quantities. **Homemade Soft Pretzels** were
  rewritten from baking ratios: a different batch, hydration, sweetener, bath and bake, and no egg wash. **Coconut
  Pecan Layer Bars** (renamed from "Magic Cookie Bars", which is a brand's name for the can-label recipe) now have a
  pre-baked brown-sugar crust, toasted pecans and flaky salt. Lava cakes and caramel corn use the dish's standard
  ratio, which nobody owns, so they stay as written.
- **Older recipes:** none of the 20 ratio flags was a real error: most counted water meant for a sauce, or skipped
  milk or tomato juice. The reviewer still fixed 3 things while reading (a list/step amount mismatch, too little time
  for brown rice, a tagline that overpromised) and the lamb biryani's simmer now allows 45–75 minutes.
- **The ratio check itself** was tuned from what the reviewer found: milk now counts as grain liquid, and pancakes and
  waffles may take about 3 tsp baking powder a cup.

## Kept as written (reviewers' recommendation)

Steak-like cube steak cooked to 160°F (it's always served well done); fried green tomatoes counting as tomato for
Avoid; coconut shrimp's sweet chili sauce required (it's in the title); the salsa verde's `grilled` tag (the broiler is
its only method); the queso's cheese-to-milk ratio; the loco moco gravy; the bobotie custard; the porcupine meatballs'
rice; the deliberate leavening in the pumpkin pancakes, sticky toffee pudding and chocolate cake; six beef and pork
bowls that cook their rice "as the package directs" (each lists the uncooked amount); and every near-duplicate the
audit flagged (different dishes).

## Riskiest on paper

Worth a second read before anything else: chicken biryani (steaming step), pupusa dough, Jamaican rice and peas,
vegetable pakoras (batter), chicken-fried steak gravy, the granola bars' binder, and the soft pretzels (new formula).
