  steps, doneness, ingredient IDs, tags, times and tagline.
- **Fixed:** 19 recipes (10 seed, 9 chicken). Every fix is small; no recipe was rewritten and no `id` changed.
- **For the owner:** 9 judgement calls (below). No recipe needs dropping.
- **No catalog changes.** No `map-entries.ts.txt` or `catalog-keys.txt`.
- **Script flags:** all 14 for my group were checked. The two that needed work are fixed (`fluffy-pancakes` had no

## Fixed
Seed recipes (`src/data/recipes.ts`):
- `black-bean-quesadillas`: the list has 2 tsp oil, but the steps used 1/2 tsp per batch over 2 batches (1 tsp) →
  1 tsp of the oil per batch, so the steps use the whole amount.
- `easy-tomato-soup`: two 28 oz cans plus 2 cups broth make about 8 1/2 cups, so "serves 4" meant 2+ cups each →
  servings 6.
- `beef-and-bean-chili`: the steps said to check the beef for 160°F after a 30-minute simmer, which is the wrong place
  for the check → moved "(160°F / 71°C)" to the browning step, where it applies.
- `overnight-oats`: Total showed 5 minutes for a recipe that needs a 6-hour chill → prepMinutes 5 → 365, the library
  convention (panna cotta, tiramisu and no-churn ice cream count their chill too). See owner item 4.
- `fluffy-pancakes`: had no dish type, so every Dish type filter hid it → `skillet` (the steps cook them in a skillet,
  and `french-toast` is tagged the same way). See owner item 3.
- `chocolate-chip-cookies`: about 1,280 g of dough scooped as rounded 2-tablespoon balls makes about 36 cookies, not 24.
  Baking one sheet at a time also takes 3 batches, not one 12-minute bake → servings 24 → 36, cookMinutes 12 → 35,
  "(about 36)" added to the scoop step, plus "Let the sheets cool before you load them again."
- `garlic-butter-shrimp`: the tagline said "done in about ten minutes", but Total is 16 minutes because peeling takes
  time → "cooked in under ten minutes" (cook time is 6).
- `peanut-butter-banana-toast`: the tagline said "two-minute" (Total is 5) and made a vague claim that it "actually
  keeps you full" → "Warm toast spread with peanut butter and topped with sliced banana, ready in five minutes."
- `apple-crisp`: step 1 butters the dish, but the only butter listed goes into the topping → the quantity is now
  "1/2 cup (1 stick), plus 1 tsp for the dish", and the step greases the dish with that 1 tsp.
- `creamy-chicken-spinach-pasta`: the pasta was drained in step 1 and then sat about 15 minutes while the chicken and
  sauce cooked → step 2 now starts "While the pasta cooks," the same way its sibling `chicken-spinach-garlic-pasta`
  does.

Chicken originals (`src/data/originals/chicken.ts`):

## For the owner
1. **Near-duplicate cluster, keep all four (recommended).** `creamy-chicken-spinach-pasta` and
   `chicken-alfredo-with-broccoli` use the same method: sear the chicken, then butter, garlic, cream, Parmesan and
   fettuccine. They differ by spinach vs. broccoli (and alfredo has more cream and cheese). Next to them are
   `chicken-spinach-garlic-pasta` (the same idea with broth, no cream) and `garlic-chicken-spinach-skillet` (the same
   chicken, spinach and garlic with no pasta). Each answers a different search (spinach vs. broccoli, cream vs. no
   cream, pasta or not), so I'd keep them. If one must go, drop `creamy-chicken-spinach-pasta`, because the alfredo is
   the fuller version of the same dish.
2. **`simple-guacamole` has no dish type, and none fits** (it's a dip). Leave it empty unless you want a "Dip/Sauce"
   dish type (guacamole, hummus, tzatziki, pico, salsas all lack one).
3. **Pancakes tagged `skillet`, so the library should match.** I tagged `fluffy-pancakes` `skillet`, like
   `french-toast`. Other groups have `banana-pancakes`, `whole-wheat-pumpkin-pancakes`, `blueberry-oatmeal-pancakes`
   and `french-crepes`, also with no dish type. Recommend tagging them all `skillet`, or reverting mine if you'd rather
   leave griddle breakfasts untyped. Waffles have no fitting type.
4. **`overnight-oats` now shows Total 6 hr 5 min.** That follows the library convention: chilling time the cook must
   plan counts. Keep it (recommended), or decide that "overnight" recipes show only hands-on time and set it back to 5.
5. **`chocolate-chip-cookies` is the standard creamed drop-cookie formula.** Its flour, butter, sugars, eggs, chips,
   soda and oven temperature match the best-known back-of-the-bag recipe, with different salt and vanilla.
   `docs/original-recipes.md` allows a basic cookie dough ratio, so I left it. I'm flagging it only because of the
   provenance rule.
6. **Optional add-ins that are cooked in, not garnishes:** red pepper flakes (`chicken-spinach-garlic-pasta`,
   `garlic-butter-shrimp`), ginger in the `honey-soy-salmon` glaze, cinnamon in `overnight-oats`, cream in
   `easy-tomato-soup`, green onion in `black-bean-quesadillas`, and jalapeño or tomato in `simple-guacamole`. Each
   recipe works without them. Avoid still sees them, and Use correctly doesn't need them. Recommend accepting "optional
   add-in" as within the rule rather than making them required.
7. **`broccoli-cheddar-soup` lists "chicken or vegetable broth" as `chicken_broth`.** A vegetarian who avoids chicken
   broth won't see it, and a pantry that has only vegetable broth won't match it. Recommend leaving it, or changing it
   to `vegetable_broth` if you want this soup to be the vegetarian one.
8. **`chicken-shawarma-bowls` needs 4 cups of cooked rice, but no step cooks it,** so Total leaves it out (like
   `vegetable-fried-rice`'s leftover rice). Recommend leaving it; the list says "cooked … rice, warm".
9. **Small things I left alone:** `chicken-cacciatore` (cook 65) and `weeknight-coq-au-vin` (cook 75) probably run
   about 10 minutes longer, because of browning 8 thighs in batches. That's within normal estimating. Kung Pao is
   called "Sichuan-style" without Sichuan peppercorns; the writer withdrew them to keep the recipe matchable. Peanuts
   have no `GRAMS_PER_CUP` entry, so `kung-pao-chicken`'s "1/2 cup" shows as 120 ml in metric (`src/logic`, outside
   my scope).
