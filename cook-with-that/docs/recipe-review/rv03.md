## Summary
- **Recipes reviewed:** 50 (25 in `src/data/originals/vegetarian.ts`, 25 in `src/data/originals/soupsSaladsSandwiches.ts`).
- **Fixed:** 6. **Questions for the owner:** 6 items (covering 9 recipes, plus two library-wide notes).
- For every recipe I checked: every "divided" or "remaining" amount against the list, ratios (liquid to grain, roux to
  liquid, dressings, binders), safe temperatures, prep and cook times against the steps, pan and blender fill,
  taglines, tags and ingredient IDs.
- The writing is careful overall. No missing ingredients, list/step mismatches, unsafe temperatures or wrong
  ingredient IDs turned up in this group. The fixes are about order, pan capacity, hot-blender safety, time and one

## Fixed
- `vegetable-lasagna-with-spinach-and-ricotta`: the 9x13 dish holds about 14–15 cups here (6 cups marinara, 1 lb
  mozzarella, 15 oz ricotta, about 1 1/2 lb sautéed vegetables, spinach, 9 noodles), which is level with the rim. It
  will bubble over onto the oven floor. → The bake step now sets the dish on a rimmed baking sheet, as
  `lentil-shepherds-pie` in the same file already does.
- `gnocchi-with-spinach-and-tomato-cream`: tagged only `skillet`, so the Pasta filter hid it. → dishTypes
  `['pasta', 'skillet']`, matching `potato-gnocchi-with-sage-butter`.
- `black-bean-and-rice-burritos`: six 12-inch burritos can't all be crisped at once in one skillet, and the rice
  alone takes about 30 minutes (boil, 15 min simmer, 10 min steam). → The last step now says "2 or 3 at a time", and
  cookMinutes goes from 35 to 45: rice 30 + warming tortillas 3 + two crisping batches about 12.
- `lentil-shepherds-pie`: the oven preheat came after the mash (step 6), which leaves about 15 idle minutes not
  counted in cookMinutes. → I moved "heat the oven to 400°F (200°C)" to the start of the potato step, which runs
  alongside the lentils.
- `chicken-tortilla-soup`: about 4 cups of hot tomato-broth mixture went into the blender in one go, with no limit on
  how full it gets. Steam can blow the lid off. → It now says "in two batches if needed so it is no more than half
  full". That matches the other blended soups in the file.
- `black-bean-soup`: same hazard. "About half the soup" is roughly 5 cups of hot liquid. → It now says "in batches so
  it is no more than half full".

## For the owner
- **Runny-yolk eggs** (`huevos-rancheros`, `sweet-potato-and-black-bean-hash-with-eggs`,
  `bibimbap-with-spinach-and-fried-egg`; 7 recipes library-wide). This is a doneness choice and normal for these
  dishes. *Recommend:* keep them. If you want to flag it, a one-line "cook a few minutes longer for firm yolks" would
  serve anyone who needs fully cooked eggs (pregnancy, young children). I made no change.
- **`falafel-with-tahini-sauce` prepMinutes 780** (audit "time" flag). It includes the 12-hour dried-chickpea soak,
  which the brief counts as a wait the cook must plan. Canned chickpeas really don't work for falafel. *Recommend:*
  keep the 780. The card will show about 13 h; if that looks off, an "overnight soak" label is an app change, not a
  recipe change.
- **Dish type for Asian noodles.** `vegetable-lo-mein` is `skillet` only, like pad thai, japchae and chicken chow
  mein, but `thai-peanut-noodles` is tagged `pasta`. *Recommend:* pick one rule. I'd keep noodles out of Pasta and
  retag `thai-peanut-noodles`, which is outside my group. Lo mein is unchanged.
- **Near-duplicate flags.** `caesar-salad` vs `chicken-caesar-pasta-salad`, `greek-salad` vs `greek-chickpea-salad`,
  and `black-bean-chili-with-bell-peppers` vs `southwest-black-bean-pasta-skillet` are each genuinely different
  dishes (plain side salad vs pasta main, village salad vs chickpea salad, chili vs pasta skillet). *Recommend:* keep
  all six.
- **`philly-cheesesteak` no-doneness-temp flag.** The steak is shaved paper-thin and cooked over high heat "until no
  pink remains". The brief allows a visual cue for thin strips. *Recommend:* no change.
- **`spinach-rice-and-feta-stuffed-peppers`** lists "2 cups cooked long-grain white rice" with no step or time for
  cooking it, the same convention as 6 other library recipes. *Recommend:* keep it. Optionally add "(from about 2/3
  cup uncooked)" so a cook without leftovers knows how much to make.
