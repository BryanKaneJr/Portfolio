# rv04 review: breakfast, sides and appetizers (`breakfastSides.ts`) and baking (`baking.ts`)

## Summary
- **50 recipes reviewed**: 25 in `src/data/originals/breakfastSides.ts` and 25 in `src/data/originals/baking.ts`.
  I read each one as a recipe tester would, checked every baking formula (flour, fat, sugar, eggs, leavening per
  cup, pan size, oven temperature and time), and rendered each in metric mode.
- **9 fixed**, **5 questions for the owner**. No catalog additions, so there is no `map-entries.ts.txt` or
  `catalog-keys.txt`.
- The baking formulas are sound. Every divided amount in the lists adds up to what the steps use, and every egg,
  meat and custard dish has a safe doneness cue (egg dishes 160°F, wings 165°F, mousse yolks cooked to 160°F, custards
  boiled 1 minute). The problems I found were yields, pan sizes, one step order, one tag and one tagline.
- `npm run check` passes after the changes (548 recipes, 735 tests).

## Fixed
- `breakfast-burritos`: the filling (1 lb potatoes, a can of beans, 6 eggs, pepper, onion, 1 cup cheddar) comes to
  about 7 1/2 cups, nearly 2 cups per burrito. That is too much for a 10-inch tortilla to roll. → Changed to 6 tortillas
  and 6 servings, step 6 now divides by sixths ("a sixth of the cheddar" instead of 1/4 cup), and the searing step says
  "in batches if needed". I also added the 165°F / 74°C reheat target to the freezer step, which only said "until hot in
  the middle".
- `baked-berry-oatmeal`: the tagline said "Reheats well all week", but the last step says leftovers keep 4 days. →
  Tagline now ends "Leftovers reheat well for four days."
- `classic-waffles`: cookMinutes was 25, but the batter (about 4 1/2 cups) makes 8–9 waffles at the step's 1/2 cup
  each, and each takes 4–5 minutes in one iron. → cookMinutes 35.
- `creamed-spinach`: it was tagged Skillet, but it is cooked start to finish in one large pot (the spinach is wilted in
  the pot, then the sauce is made in the same pot). → dishTypes `['one_pot']`, the same tag as the library's other
  pot-cooked sides (rice pilaf, cilantro lime rice).
- `spinach-artichoke-dip`: the mixture is about 4 cups (1 L) before the cheese on top, and that fills a 1-quart dish
  or standard 9-inch pie plate to the rim, so it bubbles over. → "a 1 1/2-quart baking dish or 9-inch deep-dish pie
  plate".
- `peanut-butter-cookies`: about 820 g of dough rolled into the stated 1 1/4-inch balls (about 19 g each) makes about
  40 cookies, not 30. → servings 40. cookMinutes went from 24 to 36 for three sheets baked one at a time.
- `snickerdoodles`: this is the classic 2 3/4-cup formula. About 990 g of dough in 1 1/4-inch balls makes about
  4 dozen, not 36. → servings 48. cookMinutes went from 30 to 40 for four sheets baked one at a time.
- `banana-pudding`: the custard from 3 cups of milk, a full 11-oz box of wafers, 5 bananas and 1 1/2 cups of cream
  whipped on top come to about 2.6 L. The step called for a 2-quart (8-by-8-inch) dish, which holds about 1.9 L. →
  "a 3-quart dish, such as a 9-by-13-inch baking dish or a trifle bowl".
- `pumpkin-pie`: step 3 said "Freeze for 15 minutes while the oven heats", but the oven wasn't turned on until step 4.
  A cook following the steps in order would wait an extra 15 minutes or so. → Step 3 now says "while you heat the oven
  to 400°F (200°C)", and that sentence is gone from step 4.

## For the owner
1. **`classic-waffles` needs a waffle iron.** That goes beyond the "oven, stovetop, sheet pans and a blender at most"
   line in `docs/original-recipes.md`, and the original writer flagged it too. **Recommend keep:** a waffle iron is
   common US kitchen equipment, and without this recipe the library has no waffles.
2. **Seven recipes have no dish type, and none of the current types honestly fits them:** `classic-waffles`,
   `deviled-eggs`, `tomato-bruschetta`, `pico-de-gallo`, `banana-pudding`, `chocolate-mousse` and `chocolate-pudding`.
   A Dish type filter will hide all seven. I left them alone. **Recommend** you decide library-wide whether to add a
   type or two (for example "Dip/Salsa" for pico, guacamole, hummus and tzatziki, and "Chilled dessert" for the
   puddings, mousse and panna cotta). That is a schema change, outside this review. One borderline case: by the
   library's own convention avocado toast and peanut butter banana toast are tagged Sandwich/Wrap, so
   `tomato-bruschetta` could take `sandwich` too. I left it untagged because a bruschetta appetizer under
   "Sandwich/Wrap" seemed more surprising than helpful.
3. **`avocado-toast-with-fried-egg` serves a runny yolk.** That is lightly cooked egg, which USDA guidance advises
   against for anyone at risk. The step already offers a firm yolk ("cover the pan for the last minute"). **Recommend
   keep** as written. A runny yolk is the expected version of the dish.
4. **`veggie-egg-muffins` overlaps `chicken-and-broccoli-quiche-muffins`** (VA import). Both are crustless egg muffins
   with broccoli, bell pepper and cheddar. **Recommend keep both.** Ours is vegetarian with spinach and green onion,
   and the VA one is built around leftover chicken, so each answers a different search.
5. **Audit flags I looked at and left alone:**
   - `boston-baked-beans` (375 minutes) and `classic-cheesecake` (590 minutes): both totals are real time the cook has
     to plan for (quick soak, simmer and a 3 1/2-hour bake; softening, cooling in the oven and 6 hours of chilling).
   - `pico-de-gallo` was flagged "cookMinutes is 0 but the steps cook something". That's a false positive set off by
     "spoon it over tacos and grilled meats".
   - `scalloped-potatoes` was flagged as a near-duplicate of `portuguese-creamy-cod-bake`. It isn't one: the other is
     a fish dish.

   **Recommend no change** to any of these.

Notes on what I checked:
- **Leavening per cup of flour:** cakes run 1–1 1/2 tsp of baking powder. The biscuits, scones, shortcakes and waffles
  run the usual 1 1/2 tsp. Sugar cookies and crumb bars are deliberately low. Soda is balanced by buttermilk in the
  cornbread and by brown sugar and dates in the carrot cake and sticky toffee pudding.
- **Water to grain:** pilaf is 1 : 1.83 with the rice toasted. Cilantro lime rice is 1 : 1.5 with the rice rinsed.
  Both are covered for 15–18 minutes and then rest 10.
- **Pan sizes and yields:** all match their quantities apart from the fixes above.
- **Metric mode:** every recipe renders cleanly.
