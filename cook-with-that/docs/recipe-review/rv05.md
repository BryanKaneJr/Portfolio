# rv05 review: world, weeknight, and sides and appetizers originals

## Summary
- **Recipes reviewed:** 61 (20 in `world.ts`, 21 in `weeknight.ts`, 20 in `sidesAppetizers.ts`). I read every one
  in full and checked the audit flags for 10 of them.
- **Fixed:** 3 recipes, plus a stale code comment in `weeknight.ts`.
- **For the owner:** 5 items. None of them is a recipe I'd drop.
- No catalog changes, so there is no `map-entries.ts.txt` or `catalog-keys.txt`.
- I checked every ratio, divided amount ("the remaining 1/4 tsp" and the like), doneness temperature, marinade,
  rest, chill and pan size against the ingredient list. Apart from the fixes below, all of them add up. Every
  recipe also renders cleanly in metric mode. I scanned for frying temperatures shown with fan and gas marks, for
  "an" in front of a converted number, and for solids shown in ml.
- `npm run check` passes: 548 recipes, 735 tests.

## Fixed
- `chicken-katsu-with-rice`:
  - **Oil depth didn't match the list.** The list gives about 1 1/2 cups of oil, but the step said to fill a
    "large" skillet 1/2 inch deep. In a 12-inch pan that takes about 2 1/2 cups, so the cook would run short.
  - **Change:** the step now names a 12-inch skillet and says the oil should be about 1/4 inch deep, which matches
    1 1/2 cups. That depth is a normal shallow fry for 1/2-inch cutlets that are turned once.
  - **Metric display:** "oil" used to sit more than 60 characters before "350°F", so `displayStep` showed the frying
    temperature like an oven ("175°C (155°C fan, gas 4)"). The step now says "Heat the oil … to 350°F", which
    displays as plain "175°C". A scan of the whole library found no other recipe with this problem.
- `seven-layer-dip`:
  - **Dish too small.** About 7–8 cups of layers (a 16-oz can of beans, guacamole from 3 avocados, 1 cup sour
    cream, 1 cup salsa, 1 1/2 cups cheddar and the toppings) won't fit in a standard 9-inch pie dish, which holds
    about 4 cups.
  - **Change:** the step now says "a square 8-inch dish or another shallow 2-quart dish".
- `crab-cakes`:
  - **Pan too crowded.** Eight 3-inch cakes "leaving space between them" don't fit in one 12-inch skillet. A
    beginner would crowd the pan and have trouble browning and turning the cakes.
  - **Change:** they now cook in two batches of 4, each with half the butter and oil, and the cook wipes out the
    pan between batches. `cookMinutes` goes from 10 to 20.
- `chicken-and-wild-rice-soup` (comment only, no recipe change): I removed the 10-line comment above the recipe. It
  said the soup was "held back" and gave steps to add it, but commit 39b77113 already added it, along with the
  `wild rice` map entry and the test change.

## For the owner
1. **`seven-layer-dip` and `shrimp-cocktail` have no dish type.** None of the nine types honestly fits a no-cook
   layered dip or chilled poached shrimp, so any Dish type filter hides them. **Recommendation:** leave them
   untagged rather than force a wrong type. If more no-type appetizers pile up, consider an "appetizer/dip" dish
   type. The audit also flags the dip's `cookMinutes: 0`. The only "cooking" is an optional 30-second microwave to
   loosen the beans, so I left it at 0.
2. **`rice-and-vermicelli-pilaf` vs `rice-pilaf`** (breakfastSides): both are a butter-toasted long-grain rice
   pilaf in chicken broth. The first gets its character from vermicelli browned in butter (the Lebanese and Armenian
   style), and it is the library's only exact match for rice plus pasta. **Recommendation:** keep both.
3. **`jollof-rice`: cook-test it first.** It uses 2 cups of parboiled rice, 3 cups of broth and the reduced tomato
   and pepper base, steamed under foil. Parboiled rice brands differ in how much liquid they need, and the recipe
   allows only one 1/4-cup top-up of broth. **Recommendation:** after cooking it, either confirm the amounts or
   change step 8 to "sprinkle over 1/4 cup broth at a time until tender".
4. **Near-duplicate flags, all checked and all different dishes. Keep them all:**
   - `chana-masala` vs `chicken-tikka-masala`
   - `chicken-souvlaki-with-pita` vs `lamb-gyros-with-tzatziki`
   - `chicken-saltimbocca` vs `chicken-piccata`
   - `southwest-black-bean-pasta-skillet` vs `black-bean-chili-with-bell-peppers`
   - `chicken-caesar-pasta-salad` vs `caesar-salad`
   - `one-pot-chicken-and-orzo-with-spinach` vs `greek-lemon-chicken-and-rice`: the closest pair, since both are
     lemony chicken with spinach and a starch. One is boneless thighs and orzo simmered on the stovetop with
     Parmesan; the other is bone-in thighs baked on rice.
   - `aloo-gobi` vs the VA's `indian-spiced-cauliflower-and-peas`: unflagged. The VA dish has peas and no potato or
     tomato.
5. **Cook times that run a little short** (not changed, because they're estimates rather than errors):
   - `chicken-spaghetti`: 65 minutes listed, about 80 if you add up the steps.
   - `king-ranch-chicken-casserole`: 75 listed, about 90.
   - `avgolemono`: 50 listed, about 60, since 8 cups of broth take a while to boil.

   **Recommendation:** raise them if the app ever filters or sorts by time.

The two doneness flags are fine as written:
- `ropa-vieja` is flank steak braised 2–2 1/2 hours until it shreds, far past 145°F.
- `japchae` is thin beef strips cooked "until browned and no longer pink", which the brief allows.

No recipe in this group uses a medium-rare or otherwise under-safe doneness.
