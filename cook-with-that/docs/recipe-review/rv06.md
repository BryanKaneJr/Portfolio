# rv06 review: round-4 baking and breakfast originals + first 24 VA imports

Base `48cfc51e`. `patch.diff` touches `src/data/imported/va.ts` only (no catalog changes, so no map or catalog-key
files). `npm run check` passes: typecheck, validator (548 recipes, 380 ingredients) and 10 suites / 735 tests.
Prettier-clean.

## Summary

- **Reviewed:** 44 recipes (20 originals in `bakingBreakfast.ts`, 24 VA imports from `va.ts`). Each VA recipe was
  compared line by line with its PDF text in `content/import/va/recipes.json`.
- **Fixed:** 4 recipes (all VA).
- **Questions for the owner:** 7 items, none of them blocking.
- **Baking formulas:** all 15 baked originals checked (flour, fat, sugar, eggs, leavening, liquid, pan size, oven
  temperature and time). All are within normal home-baking ranges, so no formula needed a change:
  - Red velvet: 1/2 tsp baking soda to 1 1/4 cups flour, balanced by the buttermilk, vinegar and natural cocoa.
    It's the classic formula at half size, for 12.
  - Soda bread: 1 tsp baking soda to 500 g flour.
  - Muffins: lemon has 1 tsp baking powder per cup plus baking soda for the sour cream and lemon; apple has
    1 1/4 tsp per cup.
  - Pastry: 2 1/2 cups flour to 1 cup butter for the pie crust. The Bakewell has a 0.6 butter-to-flour sweet
    shortcrust and a standard frangipane.
  - Focaccia: 83% hydration. Biscotti: stiff dough. Gingerbread: 1 tsp baking soda to 3 cups flour plus molasses.
  - Tres leches: separated-egg sponge at 1 cup flour to 5 eggs in a 9-by-13.
  - Batter volumes fit their pans (12-cup tin, 9-by-5 loaf, 9-inch tart and pie, 8-, 9- and 9-by-13-inch pans).

## Fixed

- `garlicky-mashed-potatoes`: the lemon juice was simmered in the half-and-half, which curdles it (this was in the
  source PDF too). → The half-and-half, garlic and salt are warmed on their own, and the lemon juice goes into the pot
  with them off the heat. The quantities are unchanged.
- `hash-brown-vegetable-frittata`: the "no oven-safe skillet? use an 8-inch dish" alternative came in the bake step,
  after the cook had already poured the eggs into the skillet. → It is now its own step before the eggs go in. The
  dish is greased with the listed cooking spray or oil, now described as "for the skillet or dish".
- `ginger-blueberry-crisp`: it was tagged `bake` only, while the library's other fruit desserts (Apple Crisp, Peach
  Cobbler) are `baked_goods` + `bake`. → Added `baked_goods`, so a Baked goods filter finds it with them.
- `shrimp-and-broccoli-pasta`: the tagline said "Dinner in about 25 minutes", but prep 10 + cook 20 shows as 30 on
  the recipe page. → Changed it to "about 30 minutes".

## For the owner

1. **The seven recipes with no dish type stay empty: none of the nine types fits them honestly.** They are Churros,
   Crispy Rice Treats, Fudge, No-Bake Peanut Butter Bars, Panna Cotta, Whole-Wheat Pumpkin Pancakes and Garlicky
   Mashed Potatoes (the audit's `no-dish-type`).
   - Five are no-bake or fried desserts. The pancakes and the mash are a breakfast and a side.
   - The mash uses a pot plus a small saucepan, so `one_pot` would be a stretch.
   - Recommendation: if the owner adds a "dessert/no-bake" type, the five desserts fit it. Pancakes and sides stay
     without a type.
2. **Several VA recipes add no salt at all.** The library keeps the VA's low-sodium choices on purpose, so I didn't
   change them. These three would taste notably flat:
   - Unstuffed Cabbage Rolls: no-salt tomatoes and sauce, no cheese.
   - Spinach and Mozzarella Egg Bake: 12 eggs, no salt.
   - Shrimp and Broccoli Pasta: unsalted pasta water, and only 2 tbsp Parmesan.

   Lasagna Soup is similar (no-salt broth, tomatoes and sauce; only the ricotta adds any). Recommendation: allow a
   "salt to taste" line on VA imports, or accept them as they are.
3. **Near-duplicate:** `chicken-and-broccoli-quiche-muffins` (VA) and `veggie-egg-muffins` (original) are both egg
   cups with broccoli, bell pepper and cheddar. The VA one adds cooked chicken and onion.
   - Recommendation: keep both. The chicken makes it a different pick for chicken + eggs, but it's the owner's call.
4. **Cook-test, Cherry Pie:** with fresh sweet cherries, 1/4 cup cornstarch boiled in only 1/2 cup juice sets to a
   stiff paste before it is folded into 6 cups of fruit. The writer flagged it too.
   - Recommendation: test it with fresh cherries. If it's gluey or lumpy, toss the cornstarch with the cherries and
     sugar instead.
5. **Cook-test, Unstuffed Cabbage Rolls:** regular brown rice simmered in acidic tomato sauce can take longer than
   the 40–45 minutes given. The step does say to cook "until tender" and to add water as needed.
6. **Cook-test, Whole-Wheat Pumpkin Pancakes:** 1 tbsp baking powder to 1 cup flour (the source's amount) is high
   even for pancakes, where about 2 tsp per cup is typical, and may taste slightly bitter. I kept it because it isn't
   clearly wrong. Drop it to 2 tsp if testers notice it.
7. **Runny yolks, Chilaquiles:** the fried eggs are cooked "with the yolks as runny as you like", as for other fried
   eggs in the library. No change. Mention it only if the owner wants an egg-safety note across the library.

Audit flags I looked at and left alone:
- `vanilla-panna-cotta` and `cherry-pie` "time": the 6-hour chill and the 4-hour cool are real waits the cook must
  plan.
- `apple-turnovers` "unused flour": the flour is used, as "lightly floured counter".
- `garlicky-mashed-potatoes` "vague quantity": "just enough cold water to cover" is the right instruction for
  boiling potatoes.
