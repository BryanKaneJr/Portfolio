## For the owner

1. **`minestrone-soup` has no salt at all** in about 16 cups of soup (11 cups water, no broth, no-salt-added tomato
   paste and tomatoes, low-sodium beans). The NHLBI source has none either, and the file header says to keep NHLBI's
   low-sodium choices, so I left it. It will taste flat. Recommendation: add `salt, 1 tsp` (about 1/8 tsp a bowl,
   still low-sodium) or at least "taste and season" in the last step; your call, since it overrides the source.
2. **`red-beans-and-rice` will be brothy.** 1/2 cup instant brown rice simmers covered in 1 3/4 cups broth, about
   three times the liquid instant rice absorbs, so roughly a cup of broth is left when the beans go in. That's the
   source's ratio and it reads as intended (saucy, 2-cup servings), so no change. If the cook-test finds it soupy,
   make the last 5 minutes uncovered. (The display name says "instant" clearly, which matters: regular brown rice
   would still be hard after 15 minutes.)
3. **`carrot-muffins` leavening is on the high side:** 2 tsp baking powder + 1/2 tsp baking soda for 1 1/4 cups flour
   + 1/2 cup oats, with only 1/2 cup yogurt to neutralize the soda. That's about double the usual rule, though within
   carrot-cake norms, and it's the VA's formula. Cook-test for a soapy aftertaste or sunken tops. If so, cut the
   soda to 1/4 tsp.
4. **Three recipes still have no dish type** because none of the nine honestly fits: `sesame-ginger-chicken-kebabs`
   and `air-fryer-chicken-drumsticks` (grilled/air-fried pieces, not a bake or skillet) and `blueberry-oatmeal-pancakes`
   (same as Fluffy, Banana and Whole-Wheat Pumpkin Pancakes). If the owner wants every recipe reachable under a Dish
   type filter, that needs a new type (e.g. "Grill" or "Griddle/Breakfast"). It's a product decision, not a recipe fix.
5. **`grilled-tuna-with-chickpea-and-spinach-salad`** cooks the 3-oz tuna steaks to 145°F / 63°C (opaque, flaking),
   as the source and the library's fish rule require. Many cooks serve tuna seared rare. Recommendation: keep 145°F
   for consistency with the safety rules. If you'd allow a "rare if very fresh" note, it's a doneness choice like
   medium-rare steak.
6. **`green-bean-salad-with-cranberries-and-almonds`** steams 2 lb of green beans in one steamer basket. Many baskets
   hold about half that in a layer that steams evenly. Cook-test; if it's crowded, add "in two batches if your basket
   is small" to the steaming step.

Smaller cook-test notes, no action needed: `chicken-picadillo` is 12 oz chicken for 6 servings (the source's portion;
it suggests rice and black beans alongside). `turkey-and-beef-meatballs-with-spaghetti` says level 1 1/2-tbsp scoops

va2 notes flag it too).
