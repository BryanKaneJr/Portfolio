- **39 recipes reviewed, 14 fixed, 9 with questions for the owner.**

- No unsafe temperatures or marinades left. Every raw-meat basting sauce is split or discarded, and the seafood,

  other no-dish-type flags are below.

## Fixed

- `huli-huli-chicken-skewers`: step 1 said to heat "a grill or the broiler" and also "the oven to 350°F", which a cook
  with one oven can't do at once → a grill plus the oven at 350°F, or with no grill the broiler, then turn the oven
  down to 350°F to finish.
- `couscous-with-carrots-walnuts-and-raisins`: no dish type → `one_pot`, as the library tags its other one-saucepan
  grain sides (Rice Pilaf, Cilantro Lime Rice, Rice and Vermicelli Pilaf).
- `asparagus-with-lemon-sauce`: the last step said about 1 1/2 tsp of sauce per portion, but the juice of a whole lemon
  plus 2 tbsp mayonnaise makes about 4–5 tbsp, so half would be left over → "drizzle the lemon sauce evenly over it".
- `rainbow-fruit-salad`: step 1 said to add the bananas "just before serving" inside the step that combines the fruit
  ahead, so the bananas had no clear point in the order → the bananas move into the final step, before the dressing.
- `fresh-lumpia`: 8 rolls of "about 2 tablespoons" use 1 cup, but the filling (about 3 cups raw: 1/2 cup each of pork,
  shrimp, cabbage, green beans and carrot plus 1/2 cup celery and jicama) cooks down to about 2 cups, so half went
  unused → "an eighth of the filling (about 1/4 cup)".
- `stir-fried-orange-beef`: "4 portions (about 2 cups each)" was the book's figure; 12 oz beef plus about 3 3/4 cups of
  vegetables makes about 5–6 cups → "about 1 1/2 cups each".
- `greek-style-flank-steak-with-yogurt-sauce`: "marinate for at least 20 minutes at room temperature" sets no limit on
  counter time → "20–30 minutes at room temperature, or up to 24 hours in the refrigerator".
- `lomo-saltado`: the tagline said "garlicky sirloin strips", but the recipe has 1 clove of garlic for 1 1/2 lb of
  beef → "Peru's beef stir-fry: sirloin strips with garlic, a splash of vinegar, onions and tomato, spooned over boiled
  potatoes."
- `moroccan-chicken-stew-with-couscous`: "1 1/2 lb, about 8 pieces" of skinless bone-in thighs and drumsticks doesn't
  add up (4 thighs and 4 drumsticks weigh about 2 lb), and the last step serves 2 pieces per person → "about 2 lb
  (900 g): 4 of each" (the book's intent: 2 split legs per serving). Also brown it in two batches if the pan is
  crowded. Spices and liquid left as they are.
- `turkey-mole`: the step said "the cloves" right next to a garlic clove (audit: unused "ground cloves") → "the ground
  cloves".
- `black-bean-and-chicken-stuffed-pitas`: the vegetables were cooked only in the microwave (beyond the
  oven/stovetop standard), with water the list didn't include → a covered saucepan with 2 tbsp water for 3–4 minutes
  (microwave kept as the alternative), and water added to the list.
- `bowtie-pasta-with-chicken-broccoli-and-feta`: cooked chicken was reheated with no temperature (audit flag) →
  "heated through (165°F / 74°C)", as leftovers should be.
- `jamaican-jerk-chicken`: the line is indexed as `jalapeno` but named "Scotch bonnet or jalapeño" first → "jalapeño, or
  Scotch bonnet for more heat" (as in the curried chicken), so the ID matches the first-named pepper.
- `brazilian-style-spinach-rice-bake`: "frozen chopped spinach" was indexed as fresh `spinach`, but the catalog has
  `frozen_spinach` (whose alias is exactly "frozen chopped spinach", and the VA recipes use it) → `frozen_spinach`,
  with the steps saying "frozen spinach" so the step check reads it as the listed item. See the first owner question.

## For the owner

1. **`brazilian-style-spinach-rice-bake` no longer answers a "Spinach" search.** Indexing it correctly as frozen
   spinach takes away one of the spinach + rice matches the books worker added it for. That's the catalog's fresh vs
   frozen rule working as intended. Recommendation: keep the fix. If frozen spinach should count for "Spinach",
   decide that for the whole catalog, not per recipe.
2. **`green-chile-chicken-stew`: use thighs.** The 1 1/2-hour simmer suits thighs. Breasts (which the list allows)
   turn dry and stringy, not "very tender". Recommendation: make the line "boneless, skinless chicken thighs" and
   drop breasts. I didn't change it because the source allows either. It has no internal temperature, and after
   90 minutes of simmering 1-inch cubes it doesn't need one.
3. **`apple-coffee-cake`: check the baking soda in the test bake.** 1 1/2 tsp soda for 2 1/2 cups flour is about 2.4
   times the usual 1/4 tsp per cup, and the only acid is the apples and raisins. That's normal for old-style "fresh
   apple" cakes, and it's NHLBI's tested amount, so I left it. If the cake tastes soapy or metallic, or browns too
   dark, cut it to 1 tsp.
4. **`lomo-saltado`: keep the name?** NHLBI's Latino book calls it "Stir-fried Beef". It has no soy sauce, ají or
   fries, and comes on boiled potatoes. A Peruvian cook would call it a light lomo saltado at best. The tagline now
   describes exactly what it is. Recommendation: keep it. If you want to be strict, rename it "Peruvian-Style
   Stir-Fried Beef with Potatoes" (the id can stay).
5. **`bowtie-pasta-with-chicken-broccoli-and-feta` needs 1 cup of cooked chicken but doesn't say how to cook it.** The
   book points to an appendix. Mexican Lasagna and the stuffed pitas have an "if the chicken is raw, bake it" step.
   Recommendation: add the same step here (8 oz raw breast at 400°F for 20–25 minutes to 165°F) if you don't want
   it to depend on leftovers.
6. **No dish type for 7 recipes, because none honestly fits:** `huli-huli-chicken-skewers`,
   `greek-style-flank-steak-with-yogurt-sauce`, `turkey-mole` and `ginger-soy-grilled-pork-tenderloin` (grilled or
   broiled mains; the library leaves satay, kebabs and other grilled mains untagged too),
   `strawberry-banana-yogurt-parfaits`, `asparagus-with-lemon-sauce` (steamed) and `fresh-lumpia` (fresh rolls). If
   every recipe should appear under some dish type, the type list needs a "grill" (and maybe "no-cook") type. That's
   a product decision.
7. **Doneness: `greek-style-flank-steak-with-yogurt-sauce` goes to 145°F** (medium, the USDA line), as the library
   does throughout. Flank steak is often served medium-rare (130–135°F). Leave it unless you decide to allow
   medium-rare steaks library-wide.
8. **Overlap: `cornflake-crusted-baked-fish` and the VA's `crispy-baked-cod`** are both crumb-coated baked cod. The
   coatings differ (cornflakes and buttermilk vs cornmeal, flour and Parmesan with egg). Recommendation: keep both.
   Drop one if you'd rather not have two crusted baked cods. Also, the sweep worker dropped the source's "2 drops hot
   sauce" because the catalog had no `hot_sauce` then. It does now, so it could go back. I left it out: it changes
   nothing you'd taste, and a required hot sauce would hide the recipe from pantries without it.
9. **`black-bean-and-pepper-wraps` are very full.** About 1 1/8 cups of filling goes into each 8-inch tortilla (the
   book's amounts). If the test cook can't roll them, use 10-inch tortillas. I didn't change it.
