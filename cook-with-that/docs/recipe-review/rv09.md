## Fixed
- `spanish-tortilla`: step 3 said to spoon 1 tbsp of oil out of the skillet while the potatoes were already frying
  in it → step 2 now sets 1 tbsp aside first and step 3 heats that "reserved" oil. This is a clarity fix only.
- `menemen`: thyme and paprika were `opt()` but get stirred into the eggs, so they're seasoning, not garnish →
  now required (both are kitchen staples) and the step no longer says "if using". Follows the house rule that
  optional means garnish or serve-with only.
- `cheesy-pasta-bake`: "a baking dish" had no size for 12 oz pasta plus 2 1/2 cups sauce → "9-by-13-inch".
- `hamburger-patties`: optional onion and garlic were mixed into the beef (not a garnish), and they contradicted
  the tagline ("seasoned with just salt and pepper"). The source title is "all beef" → I dropped both lines and
  the "if using" clause.
- `brown-butter-cinnamon-sugar-biscuits`: the cinnamon was given as "1/2 tbsp + 1 tsp" → "1 1/2 tsp + 1 tsp"
  (the step was changed to match). The oven was heated only after shaping, so the cut biscuits waited about 15
  minutes → the preheat now starts the dry-mix step, right after the butter has chilled.
- `honey-garlic-chicken`: 1/2 cup brown rice in 1 cup water, simmered 40–45 min in a small pot, is at the dry edge
  (the source just says "per the bag") → 1 1/4 cups water (2.5:1). The tagline said "A quick … dinner" but it
  takes 55 min because of the brown rice → I removed "quick".
- `cheesy-ham-and-potato-bake`: "a large baking dish" → "9-by-13-inch". Optional cayenne and thyme were
  seasonings baked into the dish → now required, at the same small amounts (1/4 tsp and 1/2 tsp).
- `cacio-e-pepe`: 2 oz Pecorino for 8 oz spaghetti, with half kept for topping, leaves only 1 oz to make the
  "creamy sauce". Common ratios are 3 oz or more for 8 oz → 3 oz (about 1 1/2 cups). The method is unchanged.
- `finnish-salmon-soup`: 1 tsp salt for roughly 2 1/2 quarts of soup made with **water**, not stock, comes out
  flat → "1 1/2 tsp, plus more to taste".
- `air-fryer-zucchini`: optional garlic and onion powder were tossed in as seasoning → now required (1/4 tsp each).
- `apple-pie`: the all-butter double crust went into a 350°F oven, from the source, for 45–55 min. That bakes a
  pale bottom crust, and the processor dough was never chilled. Changes: chill the two disks 30 min while the
  filling cools, bake at 375°F (190°C) on the lower rack for 50–60 min, and foil the edge if it browns too fast.
  `prepMinutes` 45 → 195 so the times include the chill and the 2-hour cool before slicing, as `cherry-pie` does.
- `cinnamon-rolls`: a "pinch" of salt in a 3-cup-flour yeasted dough is too little for flavour and for yeast and
  gluten control → 3/4 tsp, about 1% of the flour weight.
- `pesto-chicken-quinoa-bowls`: optional red pepper flakes went into the chicken marinade → now required (staple,
  1/2 tsp) and moved up next to the other seasonings.
- `honey-lime-pork-steaks`: "leave at room temperature for 30 minutes" → "cover and refrigerate for at least 30
  minutes and up to 4 hours" (USDA: marinate in the fridge). The used marinade is already boiled into the glaze.
- `spaghetti-carbonara`: the yolks are only cooked by the pasta's heat, and the draft then had the cook let the
  drained pasta cool for a minute first. Changes: the yolks are now "from very fresh medium eggs, or pasteurized
  eggs" (the same wording `tiramisu` uses), and the pasta goes into the yolks right away with hot pasta water, so
  the yolks thicken as much as the dish allows. See the owner note below.

## For the owner
1. **No dish type fits** for `banana-pancakes`, `air-fryer-zucchini`, `tzatziki`, `air-fryer-chicken-tenders` and
   `corn-salsa`. I left them empty rather than force one. Recommendation: decide this library-wide. Pancakes could
   honestly take `skillet` (`fluffy-pancakes`, `whole-wheat-pumpkin-pancakes` and `blueberry-oatmeal-pancakes` are
   empty too). Dips, salsas and air-fryer snacks have no type that fits.
2. **`spaghetti-carbonara`**: lightly cooked yolks are part of the dish and can't be made fully safe without
   changing it. I added the pasteurized-egg option and removed the cooling pause. Recommendation: keep it, and
   consider one general note (Settings or recipe page) for lightly cooked egg dishes: carbonara, `tiramisu`, and
   the hollandaise in `eggs-benedict`, outside my group.
3. **`cheesy-ham-and-potato-bake`**: the source uses only 1 cup cream for 2 lb potatoes in a 9×13. The Finnish
   kinkkukiusaus it comes from usually has more liquid. It should cook in 1 1/2 h at 400°F, but the top layers may
   come out dry. Recommendation: cook-test it. If it's dry, add 1 cup milk with the cream.
4. **`cinnamon-rolls`**: the filling (1 1/2 cups sugar, 1/2 cup butter, 4 tbsp cinnamon) is about twice the usual
   amount. It works but is very sweet and may leak and scorch in the pan. Recommendation: cook-test, and consider
   3/4–1 cup (brown) sugar and 2 tbsp cinnamon.
5. **Optional mix-ins kept optional:** bacon in `cheesy-pasta-bake` and walnuts in `banana-bread`. Both taglines
   say "if you like", and both recipes work without them. Strictly, the house rule says optional means garnish
   only. Recommendation: keep them optional, since the "No recipe needs Walnuts yet" results message already
   covers this pattern.
6. **`honey-lime-pork-steaks`**: the source cooks 1/4-inch steaks for 10 min in all, turning every 2 min. That's
   long for steaks that thin, and the honey may scorch. Recommendation: cook-test; 6–8 min is likely enough.
7. **`quiche-lorraine`**: it's the German Lothringer Käsetorte version (ham, Edam, folded whites; the tagline says
   so), and the crust is not blind-baked, as in the source. Recommendation: cook-test. If the base is soft,
   blind-bake it for 15 min.
8. **`chicken-pasta-casserole`**: the pasta is cooked 3 min short "to finish in the oven", but the only liquid
   is one can of diced tomatoes. Recommendation: cook-test; 1–2 min short may be safer.
9. **`apple-pie`** (fixed above): I raised the oven from 350°F to 375°F. Flagging it because it changes the
   source's temperature.
10. **`pumpkin-bread`** (low priority): the source's "1/2 tsp grated ginger" is listed among the sifted dry
    ingredients, so it may have meant ground ginger. The draft reads it as fresh. Either works; I left it.

No recipe to drop. Near-duplicate check: `cheesy-pasta-bake` and `classic-macaroni-and-cheese` differ (béchamel
versus an evaporated-milk custard), and `pate-chinois` and `shepherds-pie` are different regional dishes. Keep both
pairs.
