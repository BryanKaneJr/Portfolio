- **Audit flags:** I looked at every flag. The 4 real problems (wrong order, unclear timing, wrong amounts) were found
  by reading the recipes; none of them was flagged. What each flag turned out to be:
  - **no-doneness-temp** (beef-stroganoff, zurich-style-beef-and-mushrooms, frijol-con-puerco): the beef is thin strips
    with a visual cue, and the pork is simmered until very tender. Both are allowed by the brief.
  - **whole clove unused** (flemish-beef-and-beer-stew): step 4 does say "clove". There's no garlic in the recipe, so
    it can't be misread.
  - **time 435 min** (slow-cooker-chicken-tacos): real hands-off planning time. There's a separate owner question on
    it below.
  - **near-duplicate** (irish-beef-stew vs pot roast): chunked stew vs whole roast, so they're different dishes.
  - **no-dish-type** (8 recipes): see "For the owner".

## Fixed

- `french-onion-soup`:
  - **Oven timing.** The oven was preheated before the onions are caramelized, but the bread doesn't go in until about
    an hour later. "Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C)" is now its own step, just before the beef broth goes in. The broth
    takes about 25 minutes to come to a simmer and simmer, so the oven is hot by the time the bread goes in.
  - **Toast count.** The list calls for a whole baguette of toasts, but the last step floated "a cheese toast on each"
    of 6 bowls. It now says "float two or three cheese toasts on each and serve the rest alongside".
- `russian-crab-salad`: "Take the eggs out after 10 minutes" didn't say whether the 10 minutes start from cold
  water or from the boil. Timed from cold, the eggs come out underdone. It now says: once the water boils, simmer the
  eggs 10 minutes.
- `chicken-tomato-spinach-curry`: the step that blends 3 lb of tomatoes was a "Meanwhile" placed after the 1-minute
  curry-powder step. Blending takes longer than that, so the spices would scorch while you wait. Blending is now
  step 1, before the onions go on.
- `banana-chocolate-chunk-muffins`: there was 1 1/2 tsp baking powder for 2 1/8 cups (260 g) flour. That's about
  0.7 tsp per cup, well below the 1–1 1/4 tsp standard, for a heavy banana batter, so the muffins would be dense.
  I raised it to 2 1/2 tsp (about 1.2 tsp per cup). The source's 1 1/2 tsp is also low by Polish packet norms
  (about 2 tsp for this much flour).
- `chocolate-cake`: the cocoa line said "1 cup (100 g)", but a cup of cocoa is 80–90 g. The library itself uses
  20 g per 1/4 cup and 45 g per 1/2 cup. A US cook measuring 1 cup would get about 15% less cocoa than the recipe was
  built on. The line is now "1 1/4 cups (100 g)".
- `bell-pepper-and-cheddar-frittata`: optional prosciutto was stirred into the eggs. That makes it an add-in, not a
  garnish, which breaks "optional means garnish or serve-with only". I removed the line and its clause in step 2.
  The title, the tagline and the source's core recipe are all meat-free.
- `bolinhos-de-coco`: the mixture (300 g coconut plus sugar and eggs) makes about 40 level tablespoons. That's more
  than one 24-cup mini muffin tin, and the step gave no instruction for the extras. Added: "It makes about 40, so bake
  in batches if they don't all fit."

## For the owner

1. **`flammkuchen`: sour cream.** 1/3 cup is spread over two sheet-size bases (about 12 by 16 in each), which leaves
   only a bare smear. That's what the source asks for ("2 big spoons… barely covering"). Usual Alsatian recipes use
   roughly 1/2 cup or more of crème fraîche per base. *Recommend* 2/3 cup. I didn't change it because it's the
   author's stated style.
2. **`eggs-benedict`: lightly cooked eggs.** The blender hollandaise is warmed only by the hot butter, and the poached
   yolks stay runny. Both are standard for the dish. *Recommend* adding "(or pasteurized eggs)" to the eggs line, as
   `tiramisu` does, or making one library-wide decision on raw and lightly cooked eggs.
3. **`tiramisu`: raw eggs.** The yolks and whites are uncooked. The list already says "very fresh large eggs (or
   pasteurized eggs)". *Keep*, unless you want a stronger note in the steps for at-risk eaters.
4. **`frijol-con-puerco`: cut of pork.** It uses boneless pork loin simmered about 1 1/2 hours. Loin is lean and goes
   dry and stringy over that long a simmer. The source allows "loin or ribs", and the dish is usually made with
   shoulder, leg or ribs. *Recommend* switching to `pork_shoulder` (already in the catalog) with "ribs also work".
   That changes the ingredient, so I left it to you.
5. **`slow-cooker-chicken-tacos`: cooking time.** Boneless breasts cook 6–8 h on low (the source says 8 h). They're
   done long before that and get dry, though shredding them into the juices helps. *Recommend* 4–6 h on low or
   2–3 h on high, with cookMinutes around 300. That's a tested-doneness call, so I didn't change it.
6. **`lamb-biryani`: simmer time.** The steps simmer 1 1/2-inch chunks of lamb shoulder for 45–60 minutes "until
   tender". Shoulder often needs 75–90 minutes. The doneness cue protects the cook. *Recommend* widening the range to
   "45–90 minutes" after a cook test.
7. **`chicken-satay-with-peanut-sauce`: hard-to-find ingredient.** Massaman curry paste is jarred, but it's a
   specialty item in the US (the bc5 worker flagged this too). *Keep*, unless the bar is supermarket-only.
8. **No dish type (library-wide).** None of the 9 dish types honestly fits these recipes:
   - `bacon-cheddar-mashed-potatoes` (side)
   - `hummus` (dip)
   - `kettle-corn` (snack)
   - `eggs-benedict` (breakfast)
   - `sardine-cakes` (appetizer)
   - `tiramisu` (no-bake dessert)
   - `brigadeiros` (candy)
   - `chicken-satay-with-peanut-sauce` (grilled skewers)

   I left them empty. Today any Dish type filter hides them. *Recommend* deciding at library level whether to add
   types such as "dip/spread", "dessert" or "side", rather than forcing a wrong tag.
