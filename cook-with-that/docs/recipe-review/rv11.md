## Fixed
- `guobaorou`: the glaze's slurry was 2 tbsp cornstarch in 1/2 cup water. With the tomato paste, sugar and vinegar
  that is about 3 tbsp of starch per cup of liquid, which sets into a gluey paste instead of a glaze (the source used
  1 cup of water; the draft halved it without a note). → 1 tbsp cornstarch in the same 1/2 cup water (list "plus 1 tbsp",
  steps 2 and 4), the usual glaze ratio and the same thickness as the source. Also added the frying temperature,
  350°F (175°C), next to the existing visual cue (bc6 left it out only because of a units.ts bug that has since been
  fixed; it now shows plain 175°C in metric).
- `creme-brulee`: "Set 4 to 6 ramekins", but servings are 4 and the topping is 4 tsp sugar, "1 tsp over each custard"
  (6 ramekins would run short). → "Set 4 ovenproof ramekins (about 3/4 cup each)". The 540 ml custard fills four
  3/4-cup ramekins about three-quarters full.
- `maple-cardamom-granola`: dish type `bake` (shown as "Casserole/Bake") is wrong for granola. → `baked_goods`.
- `oatmeal-cookies`: the tagline said "from one bowl", but step 3 whisks the dry ingredients in a second bowl. →
  "Chewy, cinnamon-spiced oatmeal cookies with golden edges and soft centers. Stir in raisins or walnuts if you like."
- `limoncini`: the dough (650 g flour to 250 ml milk and 2 eggs, about 46% hydration, the source's own ratio) is very
  firm with US all-purpose flour. The step already said "work in a spoonful more milk", which wasn't in the list. →
  Milk is "1 cup (250 ml), plus 2–4 tbsp if needed". Step 1 warms "1 cup of the milk", and step 2 says the dough starts
  firm and to work in the extra milk 1 tbsp at a time.
- `black-bean-and-cheese-arepas`: the disks were "1/2 inch thick and 3 inches across". The dough (about 110 g per
  arepa) can't be both: it makes about 4 inches at 1/2 inch, or about 7/8 inch at 3 inches. → "about 3/4 inch thick and
  3 1/2 inches across", the usual arepa shape, which suits the 6–8 minutes a side.
- `italian-stuffed-vegetables`: the audit flagged the eggplant as unused. Step 2 only said "the others lengthwise". →
  "and the bell pepper, zucchini and eggplant lengthwise".
- `french-crepes`: a 10-inch pan and "about 1/4 cup" of batter per crêpe contradict "You should get about 15". The
  batter is about 5 cups, so 1/4 cup gives about 20 small crêpes. → "about 1/3 cup", the usual amount for a 10-inch
  pan, which gives the source's 15.
- `potato-gnocchi-with-sage-butter`: the flour is on the low side for an egg-free dough (1 1/4–1 1/2 cups for 2 lb of
  potatoes), and dumplings that dissolve in the pot are the classic failure. → Added one step after the water comes to
  a boil: drop in a test piece, and if it falls apart, knead in 2–3 tbsp more flour. It doesn't name "gnocchi"
  (catalog word).
- `swiss-cheese-fondue`: the last step needed a fondue burner, though step 2 allows a heavy saucepan. → Added "(No
  fondue set? Serve it from the saucepan and rewarm it over low heat, stirring, whenever it starts to thicken.)"

## For the owner
1. **`kalderetang-manok` needs canned Filipino liver spread** (a new catalog item). It thickens and flavors the sauce,
   so it can't simply be left out. *Recommendation:* keep. It's sold in Asian groceries and online, and the recipe
   fills chicken + cheddar. Drop it only if you want every recipe to be supermarket-only.
2. **`babas-feta-pasta` uses British mixed spice** and **`swiss-cheese-fondue` needs kirsch.** Mixed spice is in every
   UK supermarket but rare in the US. Kirsch is a liquor-store item. *Recommendation:* keep both. They are the dishes'
   signatures, and the fondue now works without a fondue set.
3. **`risengrod` short-grain porridge rice is on `rice`** (bc8 raised this; the miso bowls' sushi rice is the same
   case). *Recommendation:* keep it on `rice`. The catalog's Rice is generic white rice, and short-grain porridge rice
   isn't arborio.
4. **Four recipes have no dish type** because none fits: `no-churn-blueberry-ice-cream`, `lemon-granita`,
   `french-crepes` and `stoemp`. This is the same gap as the library's pancakes and mashes. *Recommendation:* leave them
   empty unless you add a dish type (say "Frozen dessert" or "Pancakes & crêpes"). That's an app decision, not a recipe
   fix.

**Cook-test watch list** (source formulas I left alone, but worth a close eye on the first cook):
- `okonomiyaki`: the batter is flour-heavy for the cabbage (250 g flour to about 4 cups of cabbage, the source's ratio
  doubled). Expect a dense, bready pancake. If it is, use 1 1/2 cups flour or more cabbage.
- `quick-apple-strudel`: 1 cup (300 g) of jam plus 3 apples in one puff pastry sheet is the source's amount. Watch for
  jam bubbling out and a soggy base.
- `lemon-granita`: only about 11% sugar, so it relies on the stirring every 2 hours to stay crystalline. Check the
  texture and the 12–20 hour time.
- `oatmeal-cookies`: all white sugar plus 1 tsp baking soda (no acid), so they may spread and turn crisper than
  "chewy".
- `limoncini`: confirm 2–4 tbsp extra milk is enough with US flour.
- `sticky-five-spice-pork-chops`: a 3-hour soak in 3/4 cup of soy sauce may make the chops very salty.
- `uzbek-beef-plov` and `valencian-style-chicken-paella`: the liquid amounts rely on the cues given ("cover the rice
  by 1/2 inch"; "sprinkle a little hot water if it dries out"). Check that the rice comes out tender in the stated
  times.
