## Description

Randomizer turns any choice into a moment. Add names or things, choose how the winner is revealed, set the odds if you want, and draw.

FOUR WAYS TO REVEAL
- Spin Wheel: sectors sized to the real odds, landing under the pointer
- Name Reel: a ticker of names that slows and settles on the winner
- Lottery Balls: numbered balls tumble until one rises out
- Mystery Reveal: a card trembles, then flips to show the result

YOUR CHOICE: REPEATS OR NOT
The Remove after selection switch sits on every draw screen. Turn it on and winners leave the pool for the session. Turn it off and anyone can come up again. Undo the last draw or restore removed entries any time.

ODDS YOU CAN SEE
- Equal Odds, custom Weights, or a Reverse Standings preset for fantasy drafts
- Every entry shows its weight and its chance, and chances update as people are removed
- History records the chance each winner had at the moment they were drawn

DRAFT ORDERS
Rank teams from worst to best, generate a complete weighted draft order, and reveal it from the last pick to the first.

MADE FOR REAL USE
- Save lists and pick up where you left off
- Paste a whole roster at once, up to 200 entries
- Several winners in one tap, with or without repeats
- Presenter mode for a room or a stream
- Share results as text or an image
- Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.
