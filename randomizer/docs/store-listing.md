## Description

Draft Lottery turns your league's draft order into an event. Enter last season's standings, choose the lottery format your league uses, and reveal the order pick by pick while everyone watches.

THREE WAYS TO RUN IT
- Pro Lottery: the pro basketball format. Teams that missed the playoffs draw for the top four picks, the three worst share the best odds, and everyone else picks by standings
- Long Shot: everyone's in and every pick is drawn. Last place has the best odds, and even the champ has a 1% shot at #1
- Slot Draft: the draw decides who chooses first, then each team picks its own draft slot

ODDS EVERYONE CAN CHECK
- Each team's exact chance at the #1 pick
- Each team's exact chance of landing a top-four pick, worked out precisely
- Every pick shows how far the team moved from its finish

THE REVEAL
- The whole order is drawn and saved the moment you run it
- Count down from the last lottery pick to #1 with numbered lottery balls or flipping team cards
- Presenter mode for a TV, a projector or a video call

NOTHING TO HIDE
- Every run is numbered. Run it again and everyone sees Run #2
- Share the final order as text or an image, with the odds and the run number

MADE FOR COMMISSIONERS
- Leagues of up to 32 teams; paste your standings straight from your league site
- Save every league and use it again next season
- Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.
