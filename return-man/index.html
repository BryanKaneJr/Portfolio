How to play

JoystickDrag the center circle (or use WASD / arrows) to steer. Let go and you coast to a stop instead of halting, so you can hit a move and grab the stick again. ▲ Hurdle · ILeap over a diving tackler. Hold your speed through it. ◀ ▶ Roll · J / LRoll left or right out of a tackle. You keep your momentum. ▼ Back · KJump back. The tackler overruns you and you can cut the other way. NKneel for a touchback at the 25 when you catch it in the end zone. Esc / PPause.
  • Get under the gold ring to catch the kick. Miss it and the ball is loose, and the coverage wants it too.
  • Move when a tackler lunges. Defenders flash a red "!" right before they dive. Roll, hurdle or jump back then and they eat turf.
  • Watch your energy. Every move costs energy from the bar under your feet. It refills while you run.
  • Follow your blockers. Blue jerseys pick up the coverage team. Run behind them, not into the pile.
  • Stay in bounds. Stepping out ends the play. Getting tackled in your own end zone is a safety: zero yards.
  • Style pays. Hurdles, broken ankles, jump cuts and broken tackles all earn bonus coins.

Timeout

Forfeiting keeps the coins you already earned but counts the game as a loss.
