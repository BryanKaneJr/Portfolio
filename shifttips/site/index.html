ShiftTips is an iPhone app for servers, bartenders and managers who settle tips at the end of a shift. Type the tips and hours, and everyone's share is worked out exactly, down to the last cent.

What it does

  • Simple: list who worked and their hours. Tips are shared by hours.
  • Advanced: equal pools, hours times points by role, or tip-outs by percent of tips or sales, set up the way your house does it.
  • Every split reconciles. The shares always add up to exactly what came in, and you can see the working behind every amount.
  • Saved crews and history. Save the team once. Saved shifts never change, and you can add up a pay period for payroll.
  • Share it. Send a text summary, a PDF or a spreadsheet file.

Private by design

No account, no server, no ads and no tracking. ShiftTips works offline and keeps everything on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.

Good to know

ShiftTips calculates allocations from what you enter. It isn't payroll and doesn't record that anyone was paid. Check your workplace policy and the tip pooling rules where you work.
