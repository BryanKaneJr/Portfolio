Privacy policy

Effective October 11, 2026. The short version: ShiftTips collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. ShiftTips has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it includes no third-party code that collects data. The app makes no network connections. The only exception is a link to official US Department of Labor guidance, which opens in Safari when you tap it.

What the app stores on your iPhone

To do its job, ShiftTips keeps what you enter in its own private storage on your device:

  • Crews: names, optional role labels, points and pool eligibility.
  • Shifts: the date, an optional label, tips, hours, sales amounts and the amounts worked out.
  • Your settings.

This information never leaves your iPhone unless you send it somewhere yourself. It's included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or computer) like any other app's data, under your Apple settings.

ShiftTips never asks for Social Security numbers, bank details, addresses or tax information. Please don't type them into names or labels.

Sharing

Text summaries, PDF reports, spreadsheet (CSV) files and backups are created on your iPhone. They go only where you choose to send or save them, using the iOS share sheet or the Files app.

Deleting your data

In ShiftTips, go to Settings and choose Delete All Data to remove every crew, shift and setting. Deleting the app removes them too.

Children

ShiftTips is a workplace tool and isn't directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If ShiftTips ever started collecting data, the app would ask you first.
