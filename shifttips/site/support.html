Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email SUPPORT_EMAIL and include your iPhone model and iOS version. Most answers are below.

How is the split worked out?

Amounts are kept in whole cents and hours in whole minutes, so nothing is lost to rounding. Each person first gets their exact share rounded down to the cent. The few cents left over go one at a time to the people whose exact shares were closest to the next cent; an exact tie goes to whoever is higher on the crew list. The shares always add up to exactly the tips entered. On the Review screen, tap anyone to see their working.

Simple or Advanced?

Simple splits one pool by hours worked. Advanced adds equal splits, hours times points by role, tip-outs by percent of tips or sales, and separate cash and card. Switch anytime in Settings; switching never deletes anything.

Why is someone "not in the pool"?

Owners, managers and supervisors are never included in a pool in ShiftTips, and there's no override. Anyone else can be left out of a single shift, or marked not eligible in the crew. ShiftTips never decides eligibility from a role name; you set it from your workplace's policy.

Typing hours

7.5, 7:30 and 7h 30m all mean seven and a half hours. ShiftTips always shows back how it read what you typed.

Backups and a new iPhone

Your data moves with your iPhone's own backup when you set up a new phone. You can also keep a copy yourself: in Settings, choose Back Up to Files. On the new iPhone, choose Import a Backup and pick that file. Importing can add to what's there or replace it, and a damaged file never changes anything.

Saved shifts can't be edited

That's on purpose, so a shift you shared stays exactly as everyone saw it. To redo one, open it in History and choose Duplicate as New Shift.

Does ShiftTips pay anyone?

No. It calculates how tips are allocated from what you enter. It doesn't move money, withhold taxes or check legal compliance.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Works fully offline.
